# Retro app icon

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, guided by the iOS UX Design skill and Retro's existing rewind mark and clay palette. The source image was resized to an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG for the launchers.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production-ready iOS app icon for Retro, a calm personal daily board combining goals, wardrobe and a record of lived days. Name means looking back.
Primary request: create one highly polished minimal brand app icon belonging to a family of elegant geometric apps (a faceted golden diamond on charcoal; a sage telemetry arch on charcoal). Give Retro its own warm earthy identity.
Subject: exactly two substantial left-facing rounded chevron folds forming a distinctive rewind symbol. Both chevrons have equal geometry, soft precise rounded endpoints and joins, separated by a clean visible gap. Open chevron strokes, NOT filled triangular playback buttons. Left fold uses a slightly darker muted terracotta; right fold is luminous warm clay/copper-peach. Make the two shapes feel like two beautifully folded strips of paper recording yesterday and today. The paired mark is centered optically, spans about 60 percent of canvas width and 48 percent of height, bold enough to recognize at 40 pixels.
Scene/backdrop: full bleed square dark moss-charcoal background, restrained nearly imperceptible vertical gradient from #1C1F17 top to #131410 bottom.
Color palette: warm clay #D9926F as the hero, deeper terracotta #A86C52 for the left fold. Only this earth-toned accent plus dark neutral background.
Style/medium: extremely crisp, vector-like geometry; restrained subtle tonal shading on folds to add just enough depth, mostly flat, premium native Apple app visual craft. Calm, tactile, timeless. High contrast silhouette, abundant negative space, no tiny details.
Composition: one icon only, full square 1024 by 1024 image. Background fills every edge. NO rounded square outer corners, NO border, NO outer card, NO screenshot or phone mockup; iOS applies its own corner mask.
Text: none.
Avoid: lettering, wordmark, watermarks, calendars, checklists, clocks, clothing pictograms, arrows circling a clock, extra symbols, complex monograms, glossy plastic, glass transparency, metallic chrome, sparkle, glow, cast shadows, exaggerated 3D, purple or blue, outlines around the whole icon.
